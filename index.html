What is HYPERPLEX STUDIO?

Hyperplex Studio is a game with no story, goal, or rules. You are the admin over your own simulation and you can do whatever as you want.


Playing God

You control a simple 3D stick figure character by default, but as the admin, you can manipulate your simulation however you wish.

Import 2D images or 3D models, program them into objects, players, or anything with your own rules and behaviors, etc.

The only limits are the horsepower of the host PC you run this game on!


Build worlds, games, or animations

You can rig custom players and control them with your game controller or keyboard & mouse, manipulate your simulation's logic to be a game of it's own, or create whole digital worlds, whatever satifies your heart.
